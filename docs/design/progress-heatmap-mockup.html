Progress → day-cell heatmap

Same card, same scroll, same sticky task names. What changes: instead of free-floating bars, every calendar day becomes its own box in the same grid column as its date number — so a box under “3” is the 3rd. Today in this mockup = Sep 1, 2026.

1 · Before — today’s bars, and why they feel off

✕ A bar starts and ends on a column edge (midnight), but the number “3” sits in the middle of its column — so a task due on the 3rd looks like it ends half a day early. Multiply by every row and the whole chart reads shifted.

2 · After — heatmap (play with it)

Hover any box for the exact day + state. Drag/scroll sideways — the task names stay pinned. Row is taller (38px) because the boxes are the row now; no bars floating over them.
