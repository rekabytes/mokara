Before you get started

A few things
worth knowing.

Straight answers to the questions a small team is likely to ask first.

Is Mokara for teams or individuals?

Both. You can organise your own tasks in a personal workspace, or bring teammates into a shared workspace. The main focus is small teams who need a clear way to manage tasks and projects together.

Can we try it for free?

Yes. The Free plan supports up to three people per team and includes the core task features. You don’t need a credit card to create an account. Detailed plans and capacity limits belong on the separate Pricing page.

How do I bring in my team?

Your teammates create an account, then you invite them by username from the Team page. When the first invitation is accepted, your workspace becomes a team and the work is shared.

Can we run it on our own server?

Yes. Self-hosting is available for free, without the hosted capacity caps. You manage your own infrastructure and data. If you’d rather not manage a server, use the hosted service.

About this design concept

Structure informed by Basecamp (product demonstration and customer stories), Todoist for teams (outcome-led benefits and FAQs), and Linear (product-led visuals and a clear final CTA). This is a Mokara concept, not a copy of any competitor.

All four images are existing Mokara screenshots from the repository. They are older saved captures with example workspace data—not newly captured screens or customer evidence. Feedback cards are placeholders awaiting genuine, approved quotes.

Design preview only. Screenshot tabs, full-size image links, and FAQs work. Pricing, signup, login, contact, and privacy are placeholders for separate destinations; no live accounts or backend requests are created.
